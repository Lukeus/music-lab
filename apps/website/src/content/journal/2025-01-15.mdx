That old technique I learned as a kid—fighting nightmares by becoming the superhero in my dreams—it applies to everything. Creative blocks, difficult emotions, life challenges. Put positive thinking and dedicated focus towards a dream, and the outcome usually favors the dreamer. Music is just another way of dreaming solutions into existence, turning fear into melody and struggle into rhythm.
