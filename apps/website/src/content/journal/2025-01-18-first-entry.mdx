There's something about the vibrations of an acoustic guitar against your body that can't be replicated. It's not just hearing the music—it's feeling it resonate through you like a second heartbeat. Today I spent time trying to capture that physical connection in my recordings. How do you translate a feeling into something others can experience through speakers? It’s not about technical accuracy, it’s about emotional accuracy.
