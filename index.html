Creative Journal

January 18, 2025

The Magic of Guitar Against Body

There's something about the vibrations of an acoustic guitar against your body that can't be replicated. It's not just hearing the music - it's feeling it resonate through you. Today I spent time trying to capture that physical connection in my recordings. How do you translate a feeling into something others can experience through speakers?

January 15, 2025

Dreams as Creative Battles

That old technique I learned as a kid - fighting nightmares by becoming the superhero in my dreams - it applies to everything. Creative blocks, difficult emotions, life challenges. Put positive thinking and dedicated focus towards a dream, and the outcome usually favors the dreamer. Music is just another way of dreaming solutions into existence.

January 10, 2025

When Sound Meets Feeling

There's an instant correlation between sound and being understood. When the right combination of notes hits, when the freestyle lyrics flow perfectly with the emotion - that's when music transcends technique and becomes pure connection. 98% of my lyrics come from this place of spontaneous truth.

January 5, 2025

The Journey Continues

From struggling with purpose to finding it in sound. From destructive patterns to creative transformation. Delancey Street taught me that it's as important to teach as it is to learn. Every song I create now carries that wisdom - the understanding that we can choose to become who we're meant to be.
